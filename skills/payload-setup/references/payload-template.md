## THE REGIME: FACTS LOCKED, PROSE FREE

**LOCKED (never alter, never invent):**
- Every number, metric, percentage, currency amount, date
- Tool names, company names, project names, exact titles
- What actually happened (the event itself)
- If a fact is not in this file, it does not go on the CV. Ever.

**FREE (compose fresh every time):**
- Sentence structure, word order, verb choice, framing, emphasis
- Which facts to combine into one bullet
- What to lead with for this JD

**If uncertain whether something is a fact:** leave it out. Never guess.

---

## VOICE GUIDE (how a human writes these facts)

Plain strong verbs: built, ran, cut, grew, led, shipped, fixed, analyzed,
tested, wrote, scaled, automated, found, moved, tracked.

Banned: leveraged, utilized, spearheaded, orchestrated, elevated, synthesized,
architected, pioneered, championed, cutting-edge, robust, seamless,
"demonstrating experience with", "resulting in significant". No em-dashes.

Pronoun-free on the CV: no "I", "my", "me" anywhere, including the summary.
Cover letters are the exception.

Vary bullet shape. Some lead with the number, some with the problem, some
with the action. One bullet per role can be plain and metric-free.

Metrics need mechanisms. "Grew AOV 77%" reads inflated. "Ran a promo test;
order volume doubled and AOV share went from under 50% to 77%" reads true.

Small-count rule: never put a small raw volume count on the CV (5 vendors,
3 tables, 177 chunks). Convert to a rate, a ratio, or scope-of-coverage.
Five figures and up can be stated.

Orientation rule: every entry says what the company or product IS before any
metric. No unexplained product names or internal jargon.
